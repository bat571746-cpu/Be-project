double encryption · quantum + classical

Files protected by a measurement, not a guess.

Every file you encrypt here passes through two independent stages. First, a quantum circuit measures qubits in superposition to produce a seed that cannot be predicted — not by a better computer, not by more time, by the laws of physics. Second, that seed is folded into your password and used to drive AES-256-GCM, the same authenticated cipher used to protect data at rest in production systems.

01

Quantum-sourced entropy

The seed and nonce for every encryption are generated by measuring real quantum circuits on Qiskit's Aer simulator — genuine non-deterministic randomness, not a classical PRNG.

02

Hybrid key derivation

Your password and the quantum seed are combined through PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 at 390,000 rounds. An attacker needs both secrets — neither alone derives the key.

03

Tamper-evident output

AES-256-GCM authenticates as it encrypts. Wrong password or a single altered byte and decryption fails outright, instead of silently returning garbage.
